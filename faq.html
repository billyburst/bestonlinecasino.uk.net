Frequently Asked Questions

Are online casinos legal in the UK?

Yes, online casinos are legal in the UK when operated by companies licensed by the UK Gambling Commission. All casinos we recommend hold proper licensing and comply with UK gambling regulations.

How do I know if a casino is safe?

A safe casino will have a UK Gambling Commission license, use SSL encryption to protect your data, and display responsible gambling information. Check for the UKGC logo and license number, usually found in the footer of the casino website.

What is a welcome bonus?

A welcome bonus is a promotional offer for new players who sign up and make their first deposit. This can include deposit match bonuses, free spins, or a combination of both. Always read the terms and conditions, including wagering requirements.

What are wagering requirements?

Wagering requirements specify how many times you need to bet a bonus amount before you can withdraw winnings. For example, a £10 bonus with 30x wagering means you need to wager £300 before cashing out. Lower wagering requirements are more player-friendly.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal times vary by casino and payment method. E-wallets like PayPal, Skrill, and Neteller are usually fastest (a few hours to 24 hours). Debit cards typically take 1-3 days, while bank transfers can take 3-5 days. Some casinos also have a pending period before processing withdrawals.

Do I need to verify my account?

Yes, most UK casinos require identity verification before your first withdrawal. This is a legal requirement to prevent fraud and money laundering. You'll typically need to provide proof of identity (passport or driving license) and proof of address (utility bill or bank statement).

Can I play casino games on my mobile?

Yes, all modern UK online casinos are mobile-friendly. You can play through your mobile browser or download a dedicated casino app if available. Mobile casinos offer the same games, bonuses, and features as desktop versions.

What payment methods can I use?

UK casinos typically accept debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), e-wallets (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller), bank transfers, and Pay by Mobile options. Credit cards are no longer accepted at UK casinos due to gambling regulations.

Are casino games rigged?

No, legitimate UK casinos use Random Number Generators (RNG) to ensure fair and random game results. Licensed casinos are regularly audited by independent testing agencies like eCOGRA and iTech Labs to verify fairness.

What is a live dealer game?

Live dealer games stream real casino tables with professional dealers to your device in real-time. You can play games like blackjack, roulette, and baccarat while interacting with dealers and other players through chat. It combines the convenience of online play with the atmosphere of a real casino.

What are free spins?

Free spins allow you to spin slot reels without using your own money. They're often included in welcome bonuses or ongoing promotions. Winnings from free spins usually have wagering requirements before you can withdraw them.

Can I set deposit limits?

Yes, all UK casinos are required to offer deposit limits as part of responsible gambling measures. You can set daily, weekly, or monthly limits on how much you can deposit. You can also use self-exclusion tools if you need to take a break from gambling.

What if I have a problem with a casino?

First, contact the casino's customer support team through live chat or email. If the issue isn't resolved, you can escalate to the UK Gambling Commission or use alternative dispute resolution services like eCOGRA or IBAS.

Do I have to pay tax on casino winnings?

No, casino winnings are not taxable in the UK. Players do not need to declare casino winnings to HMRC as the tax is paid by the casino operators.

What is RTP?

RTP stands for Return to Player and represents the percentage of wagered money a game pays back to players over time. For example, a slot with 96% RTP will theoretically return £96 for every £100 wagered over the long term. Higher RTP games generally offer better value.

Can I play for free?

Many casinos offer demo versions of slots and table games that you can play without depositing money. This is a great way to try games before playing for real money. However, live dealer games and some exclusive titles usually require real money play.
